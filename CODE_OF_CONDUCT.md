# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Growvidence to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming learning environment. People should be able to participate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or chosen learning path.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical feedback with context and actionable suggestions.
- Respect privacy; do not share another person's learning records, evidence, or contact details without permission.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and differing perspectives.
- Help keep discussions focused and accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation for behavior they consider abusive or harmful. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where doing so is safe and appropriate.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's available private contact or reporting mechanisms. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. This policy is a community standard, not a substitute for applicable law or platform rules.
