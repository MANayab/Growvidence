# Slice 4 exercise — competencies and evidence

This is a learner exercise for the maintainer to complete personally. Do not implement it on their behalf.

1. Choose one criterion from each competency and explain what would and would not qualify.
2. Add a journal entry and disclose AI use honestly.
3. Add evidence with an artifact link, criterion, context and optional journal/task links.
4. Verify that unknown/not_recorded disclosures cannot satisfy unassisted criteria.
5. Repeat a criterion in a distinct context/task at least 14 days later.
6. Compare status before and after soft-delete; review the permanent-delete confirmation.
7. Explain what competency status cannot claim (certification, job readiness or guarantees).
