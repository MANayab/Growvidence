# Growvidence — product mind

## Vision

Help people turn learning into demonstrable capability, with a clear record of what they practiced, what they can do independently, where AI helped, and what evidence supports their progress.

## Product promise

**Don't just learn. Prove your progress.**

This is an aspiration, not a guarantee of employment, certification, or skill level.

## Target personas

### IT beginner / student
Needs a simple structure for learning tasks, practice, and proof of progress. May not know how to judge whether a skill is truly learned.

### Self-taught learner / career changer
Needs to organize multiple learning tracks and produce credible artifacts that can be reviewed by another person.

### Mentor / reviewer
Needs enough context to give useful feedback without treating activity metrics as proof of competence.

## Product principles

1. **Evidence before scores.** Every competency claim should point to evidence and criteria.
2. **Independence is explicit.** AI-assisted work is valuable learning activity, but must not be misrepresented as independent performance.
3. **Progress is not a streak.** Streaks and badges may encourage consistency but never determine mastery.
4. **Learner ownership.** Provide clear access, export, and deletion controls.
5. **Accessible by default.** Keyboard, screen-reader, responsive, low-bandwidth, and localization needs are first-class.
6. **Honest uncertainty.** “Not yet demonstrated” and “needs more evidence” are valid states.
7. **Beginner-friendly, not patronizing.** Explain concepts and next actions in plain language.

## Six initial competency areas and proposed pass criteria

These criteria are draft internal rubrics. They require pilot testing and revision; they are not external certifications.

| Competency | Proposed evidence / pass signal |
|---|---|
| Independent coding | Build a small feature without AI-generated code, run it, and explain key implementation choices |
| Debugging | Diagnose and resolve three unfamiliar defects; document hypotheses, tests, and root cause |
| Architecture | Present a design, explain constraints, and defend trade-offs against alternatives |
| Testing | Independently create and run tests covering normal, edge, and failure behavior |
| Commercial validation | Obtain real customer feedback and test willingness to pay; report sample and limitations honestly |
| Technical fundamentals | Explain and safely modify an existing codebase, including relevant data/control flow |

Commercial validation is a product/business competency, not a substitute for technical skill assessment. “Pass” should require reviewable artifacts and explicit criteria, not a self-reported checkbox alone.

## Evidence record should answer

- What was attempted?
- What did the learner understand or discover?
- What was completed independently?
- Was AI used, and how?
- What artifact, repository, test result, or reviewer feedback supports the claim?
- Which competency and criterion does the evidence relate to?
- What remains uncertain or should be practiced next?

## Learning tracks to consider

Software development; SEO; AEO; GEO; structured data / Schema.org; technical SEO; content writing; analytics (including GA4 and Search Console); prompt engineering and LLM APIs; communication and other professional skills.

These are candidate tracks, not a promise that every track will ship in the MVP.

Names and sub-brand structure are unapproved concepts.

## Experience direction

- Daily / weekly / monthly planning
- Kanban and list views
- Learning journal and spaced review
- Clear evidence trail and reviewer feedback
- Dashboard, progress rings, skill radar, and reports where they improve understanding
- Gamification as optional motivation, never as a competency score
- Dark mode, responsive layouts, useful empty states, and accessible feedback

## Non-goals

- Claiming accreditation or guaranteed employability
- Equating time spent, task count, streaks, or course completion with mastery
- Presenting opaque AI-generated ratings as objective truth
- Publicly exposing private learning records by default
- Building a native mobile app before validating PWA limitations
- Adding paid tiers before market validation and license approval
